# UPI and payment fraud
In UPI fraud a scammer sends a collect request or QR code and claims the victim will receive money. Receiving money never requires entering a PIN. Entering a UPI PIN always sends money out.
Fake customer-care numbers found through search engines are a common vector. Banks never ask for PIN, CVV or OTP over phone or chat.
If defrauded, immediately call the bank, block cards and UPI, and file a complaint on the national cybercrime helpline 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in as soon as possible because early reporting improves the chance of freezing funds.
