# Passwords and multi-factor authentication
Use long unique passphrases of at least 14 characters and a password manager. Reusing passwords enables credential stuffing, where leaked credentials from one site are tried on others.
Multi-factor authentication adds a second factor. Authenticator apps and hardware security keys are stronger than SMS OTP because SIM-swap attacks can intercept SMS codes. Passkeys based on FIDO2 resist phishing because they are bound to the legitimate domain.
