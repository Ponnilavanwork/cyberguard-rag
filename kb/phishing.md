# Phishing
Phishing is a social-engineering attack where an attacker impersonates a trusted entity such as a bank, courier or government office to trick a victim into revealing credentials, OTPs or payment details.
Common signs: urgent language, mismatched sender domain, misspelled brand names in the URL, shortened links, requests for OTP or password, and unexpected attachments.
Protection: never click links in unsolicited messages, open the official website manually, enable multi-factor authentication, and report the message to the email provider or national cybercrime portal.
